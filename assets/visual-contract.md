# Visual contract and representative scene

Copy this into the project when a direction spans several assets or scenes. Fill the fields that affect the assignment and remove unused prompts.

## Assignment

- Player activity and immediate decision
- Requested scope and delivery stage
- Approved qualities to preserve
- Rejected directions and why they failed
- Open design hypothesis
- Target devices, inputs and actual display sizes

## Visual language

- Desired mood and core interactive object
- Silhouette, proportions and family differences
- Scene, character and control perspective
- Edges, stroke weight, values and light direction
- Palette roles for background, subject, action, danger and unavailability
- Materials and concentration of detail
- Type, numerals, icons and minimum reading size
- Motion at rest, during input, at an event and at results

## Atmosphere key

- Place, time, weather and the emotional change across the scene
- Three thumbnail keys with the same anchor; mark the warmest patch, sharpest edge and largest area of rest
- One material study at actual play size; how it wears, catches light and moves when handled
- One gesture, prop or ambient sound that gives this particular world a history
- Which visual cue survives in the interface, and which disappears when the scene gets busy
- Rejected key, specific difference and reason for choosing the current direction

## Representative playable moment

- Objects, states and actions included
- Input, acknowledgment, outcome and next action
- Geometry and visibility rules that must hold
- Observations that justify extending the direction
- Observations that would prompt a revision
