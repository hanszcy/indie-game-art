# Frame the verb

Watch where the player's eyes and hands spend the scene. Use that evidence to choose a camera, subject scale and density of detail. If the brief says “like a particular game,” compare the same action and perspective in both. A lantern on a winding road and a lantern above a crowded crew deck may belong to the same world but call for different compositions.

| Kind of scene | Visual question | A frame to paint or capture |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Action or defense | Where can the next threat be seen, and where does impact read? | A warm hit on a cool waterline, an enemy winding up at the edge, the defender behind a thumb. Keep the threat's silhouette when the hit peaks. |
| Hidden-role or inference | How do current, uncertain and remembered marks differ? | A single board crop with one private mark and one public clue. The observer's version needs its own truthful art layer. |
| Puzzle or construction | What appears built, movable, spent or recoverable? | The same piece anchored, lifted and seated. Repeat the edge or footprint across states to make its material feel continuous. |
| Exploration or story | How much of the world does the camera withhold? | An arrival view, the same landmark seen from another route and a quiet shot on return. Let the changed place carry memory. |
| Character selection or conversation | What relationship do posture, distance and gaze express? | Put two silhouettes in frame with the dialog panel and long names. Preserve the shoulders, face or hand that carries the emotion. |
| Shop or card draft | Where can the illustration be lush, and where does comparison need quiet? | Three offers with different costs and one long translation. Keep their frames in one material family; give the current choice the cleanest edge. |

Follow a recurring shape from the title through one ordinary scene and an ending. Test its smallest use and the busiest one. If the title is the only frame with personality, bring an authored shape or material into a commonly touched object. If everything carries the logo or signature flourish, remove it from a secondary surface and compare.

Where the desired picture conflicts with existing rules or hides information, name that constraint in the brief. For a visual polish assignment, find a treatment inside those rules. A separate mechanics redesign has different success criteria.
