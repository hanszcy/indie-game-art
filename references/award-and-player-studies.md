# Choosing what to study

A prize can lead to an unfamiliar game. A player-review aggregate can lead outside that prize list. Neither tells you why a particular frame or interaction works. Use the evidence in the last column to choose a moment worth studying. In a brief for a small team, borrow a decision the team can afford to repeat.

## Twelve editions of The Game Awards

These are the winners of the independent-game category for the completed 2014–2025 editions. Category names vary slightly across the archive. The year links are results. The craft links point to game makers, presented material or the game itself. The proposed exercises are ours.

| Edition and winner | What to study | An edit to try |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [2014](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FoNRM_sAjzQ) · Shovel Knight | Yacht Club added a small number of colors to its NES-inspired palette. A beige solved Polar Knight's sheepskin cloak, and a brown widened the range of skin tones in backer portraits. [The studio's palette examples](https://www.yachtclubgames.com/blog/breaking-the-nes/) | In a limited-palette cast, try one extra value on the specific character whose material or complexion collapses. Recheck the cast together before expanding the global palette. |
| [2015](https://gameinformer.com/b/news/archive/2015/12/03/the-game-awards-2015-winners-news-and-reveals) · Rocket League | Psyonix recommends practicing Ball Cam in Free Play because it builds awareness of the field. The camera determines which relationship can be judged while steering. [Psyonix on Ball Cam](https://www.rocketleague.com/news/five-tips-for-taking-your-game-from--potato--to--pro-?lang=en) | For a goalkeeper, frame the line from player to ball and try it on a reversal. Widen the frame if the destination disappears. Preserve a steady reference for steering. |
| [2016](https://www.famitsu.com/news/201612/02122043.html) · INSIDE | Playdead placed bounce, rim and shadow effects by hand. In the dark hall in its Steam gallery, a narrow lit floor and overlapping figures do much of the staging. [Artist's lighting breakdown](https://blog.playdead.com/articles/the_lighting_of_inside/lighting_of_inside.html) · [Game gallery](https://store.steampowered.com/app/304430/INSIDE/) | Add one local pool of light at the place where a silhouette changes meaning. Move the character through it. A permanent outline on every prop may reveal a threat too early. |
| [2017](https://www.famitsu.com/news/201712/08147682.html) · Cuphead | Studio MDHR paired hand-drawn cel animation with watercolor sets. The Beppi battle image puts a hard-edged toothy head and the player over a painted fairground. [Techniques and gameplay gallery](https://studiomdhr.com/press-kits/) · [Battle image in the Steam gallery](https://store.steampowered.com/app/268910/Cuphead/) | Give the changing silhouettes cleaner edges than the scenery. Inspect the incoming hazard, the player and the exit gap together at their smallest scale. A film-grain overlay cannot do this separation for you. |
| [2018](https://thegameawards.com/rewind/year-2018) · Celeste | The landing code distinguishes degrees of compression and gates dust separately. [Ground-collision study](indie-craft-studies.md#which-part-of-an-action-should-move) | Draw the lightest possible landing and the hardest one first. Check the next legal jump in both. |
| [2019](https://thegameawards.com/rewind/year-2019) · Disco Elysium | Anton Vill's Thought Cabinet weaves the individual thought icons into a single illustration. The player can discover and internalize thoughts, giving an inventory for ideas a visual form. [Studio introduction and artwork](https://discoelysium.com/devblog/2019/09/30/introducing-the-thought-cabinet) | When a whole collection tells a story, try designing that whole image first. Then crop three selections and see if each still reads while its neighbors recede. |
| [2020](https://thegameawards.com/rewind/year-2020) · Hades | Death brings Zagreus back to a recurring place and relationships can develop across escape attempts. Returning is an authored part of the world. [Supergiant's game page](https://www.supergiantgames.com/games/hades/) | After a failed run, let one familiar character or prop acknowledge the specific attempt. Test an immediate retry and a player lingering to look. |
| [2021](https://thegameawards.com/rewind/year-2021) · Kena: Bridge of Spirits | Ember Lab describes characters attending to the photo-mode camera and a command that draws a reaction from the group. A small change of gaze makes a posed group feel aware of the viewer. [Ember Lab's photo-mode demonstration](https://blog.playstation.com/2021/09/20/say-cheese-announcing-kena-bridge-of-spirits-photo-mode/) | Try gaze changes on a multiplayer lineup while the feet stay planted. Run it with one and eight figures. Give the change to a few attentive faces if a synchronized row feels mechanical. |
| [2022](https://thegameawards.com/rewind/year-2022) · Stray | A real cat inspired the protagonist's appearance, another supplied run and jump reference. The animator and programmer iterated together on transitions. [BlueTwelve's reference and animation account](https://blog.playstation.com/?p=367025) | Make the first paw to take weight identifiable in a small landing sprite. If it reads well in a large drawing but disappears as a token, simplify the torso and preserve the contact shape. |
| [2023](https://thegameawards.com/rewind/year-2023) · Sea of Stars | The enemy's locks communicate damage types relevant to interrupting an attack. Timed hits and the longer decision about breaking locks coexist. [Sabotage's description of the combat system](https://sabotagestudio.com/presskits/sea-of-stars/) | Give a timing cue a brief accent and the remaining requirements a stable place. Trigger the flash over a complex target and check that the next requirement is still legible. |
| [2024](https://thegameawards.com/rewind/year-2024) · Balatro | In the first Steam gallery frame, a local +10 sits by the played cards while the two colored operands and the target occupy the side rail. Joker effects can change how the hand contributes. [Gameplay gallery](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2379780/Balatro/) · [Official FAQ](https://www.playbalatro.com/faq) | In a combo game, mark the item responsible for a bonus, let a fixed tally collect it and leave the target visible. Test a low-scoring hand as carefully as a jackpot. |
| [2025](https://thegameawards.com/rewind/year-2025) · Clair Obscur: Expedition 33 | Sandfall's gameplay breakdown shows a persistent turn order and describes reactive inputs during attacks and defense. Composing for long-term planning and composing for the next contact are distinct tasks. [Sandfall's battle walkthrough](https://www.expedition33.com/post/your-first-look-at-gameplay-from-clair-obscur-expedition-33) | For a timed defense, freeze just before contact. Check where the eyes must be for the cue and which tactical fact must remain available after the reaction. Budget for one clear silhouette even with a small animation team. |

## Steam selections from different kinds of play

Sampled on November 25, 2026 using the English storefront's all-time **English Reviews**. These are rounded percentages displayed by Valve for that language and period, a selection across genres, and subject to change. They do not attribute approval to the interface, soundtrack or any other individual feature.

| Title and Steam reading | Evidence to examine | Question for the next frame |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Baba Is You · 97%](https://store.steampowered.com/app/736260/Baba_Is_You/?l=english) | The words that describe a level's rules are movable blocks in that level. [Teikari's game page](https://www.hempuli.com/baba/) | If a label *is* a piece, does it share the grid and the movement feedback of the pieces it governs? |
| [Slay the Spire · 97%](https://store.steampowered.com/app/646570/Slay_the_Spire/?l=english) | The developers recount replacing the previous enemy-move display with intent. Study the discarded interface as well as the one that shipped. [Yano and Giovannetti's walkthrough](https://arstechnica.com/video/watch/war-stories-slay-the-spire-war-stories/) | When deciding how much to block, can the player connect a threat to its owner from the ordinary combat view? |
| [Papers, Please · 97%](https://store.steampowered.com/app/239030/Papers_Please/?l=english) | The phone port preserves the downward action that makes an impression. [Stamp desk study](indie-craft-studies.md#when-should-a-control-take-over-the-screen) | Which part of this action must survive a new input device? |
| [A Short Hike · 99%](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1055540/A_Short_Hike/?l=english) | Restricting apparent resolution changes which silhouettes and boundaries can carry an entire landscape. [Creator's illustrated retrospective](https://blog.playstation.com/2021/08/05/crafting-a-tiny-open-world-a-look-behind-the-scenes-at-the-creation-of-a-short-hike/) | If the grass vanishes at phone size, do the ridge and the route still look inviting? |
| [Return of the Obra Dinn · 96%](https://store.steampowered.com/app/653530/Return_of_the_Obra_Dinn/?l=english) | The artist tested his signature rendering during camera rotation. [Dither comparisons](https://dukope.com/devlogs/obra-dinn/tig-32/) | Does a surface stay attached to the object as the camera moves? |
| [Unpacking · 93%](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1135690/Unpacking/?l=english) | An alarm clock changes its display when picked up and set down. [Developer's object notes](https://blog.playstation.com/2022/05/06/objects-tell-stories-in-unpacking-arriving-on-ps4-and-ps5-may-10/) | Can a familiar property of the object confirm the action and reveal character at the same time? |
| [Balatro · 98%](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2379780/Balatro/?l=english) | The scoring frame above separates the current contributor, calculation and target. | Where can the player still find the cause once the transient score has disappeared? |

**A comparison for a new brief.** Say a puzzle has a beautiful illustrated rulebook that players keep reopening. *Baba Is You* makes working rules visible because moving the words *is* the activity. *Slay the Spire* exposes enough upcoming intent for a player to choose a response. *Disco Elysium* gives interpretation and discovery a slower, richly illustrated space. For an irrigation game, keep today's water direction at the gate where the player decides, and give the keeper's history room in a journal. Hide the journal, change the water direction, then reopen it. Check whether the move is still predictable and whether the journal is still worth looking at.
